Ask the Consumer Rights Act 2015, Part 1, and check every answer.

Answers questions about the Consumer Rights Act 2015, Part 1 from its own text only: the best-supported sentence, word for word, with its passage and character offsets. Refuses questions outside its lane. It was folded from 5 document(s), 372 passages by Genome Fold, and it is admitted as a cell of the estate genome: its own CI re-runs the birth check on every push.

Start here: ask it

  1. Type a question about the Consumer Rights Act 2015, Part 1 below and press Ask, or press one of the example questions.
  2. A verified answer is a sentence of the source, word for word, with the passage it came from highlighted and its character positions, so you can check it. Verified means the words are really there; it does not promise the sentence is the best answer.
  3. A refused question is outside this cell's lane: too few of its words are words the source uses. A not verified answer was found but the judge would not verify it, so it is withheld.

Everything runs in this page; nothing is sent anywhere.

How it works

  1. The source is cut into passages at blank lines and headings; a heading titles the passages under it.
  2. The answerer picks the passage that best carries your question's words (BM25 word overlap, rare words counting more), then the sentence in it that carries the most of them. No language model is involved.
  3. The judge verifies the answer record only when its text is exactly the passage's characters at its positions and the passage uses enough of your question's words.
  4. The lane refuses a question when too few of its words are words the source uses. Its threshold was chosen and measured on questions generated from the source and a fixed off-topic set (below).

Using it

  • In a page: this one. Share the link; it works offline once loaded.
  • From a program or an agent: clone sjgant80-hub/consumer-rights-cell and run echo '{"question":"..."}' | node genome/act.mjs. It prints a verified record and exits 0, or a refusal and exits 1.
  • When the source changes: fold it again with Genome Fold. The new cell is written only if the birth check admits it.
  • Support: supplied by AI Native Solutions; support is agreed per deployment, in each deployment's agreement.

Limits

  • The answer is a whole sentence found by word overlap, not a short answer, and verified does not mean it is the best sentence.
  • No stemming: different word forms ("reject" and "rejected") do not match.
  • Questions that use the source's words but that it does not answer are mostly answered anyway. On a public test set, a cell folded the same way refused only about a third of such questions (the measurement).
  • This is not advice. For anything that matters, read the cited passage in the source.
